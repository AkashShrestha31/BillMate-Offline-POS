Billmate is an offline POS, shop cash register, and restaurant billing app built for retail stores, cafés, bars, and small businesses.

INVOICING & BILLING

Create sales and purchase invoices with line items, discounts, and customer tracking — a full invoice maker in your pocket. Record expenses by category, track outstanding balances (credit / udhar / khata) for every customer and supplier, and settle payments in full, partially, or in bulk. Export any invoice, bill, or receipt as PDF. No internet required.

POS FOR SHOPS & RETAIL

Fast checkout for retail shops and small businesses. Accept cash, QR, and card payments, and print receipts to any ESC/POS thermal printer. Your phone becomes a cash register.

RESTAURANT & BAR POS WITH TABLE MANAGEMENT

A complete restaurant POS with visual table layout. Take orders by table or takeaway, send them to the kitchen (KOT), and follow every dish from order to delivery. Includes a dedicated liquor catalogue built for bars.

INVENTORY & STOCK MANAGEMENT

Track stock across your shop, store, or restaurant. Reconcile physical counts against records, fix discrepancies on the spot, bulk-upload stock via CSV or Excel, and export stock reports as PDF.

STAFF, PAYROLL & ATTENDANCE

Keep staff profiles, record daily attendance, and generate payroll automatically from working days and salary, with advance deductions handled for you. Export payslips as PDF and manage leave approvals.

REPORTS, ACCOUNTING & ANALYTICS

Profit and loss statements, customer and supplier account statements, payment summaries, and sales-versus-expense charts by day, week, month, or custom range. A daily cash summary gives you a clear end-of-day picture.

OFFLINE FIRST — ALWAYS ON

Sales, expenses, inventory, staff, and cash tracking all run on your device and survive restarts. No connection, no interruption.

ROLE-BASED ACCESS & SECURITY

Set permissions per role, from cashier to manager, with granular control over invoices, returns, and edits. Your business data stays on your device.

MULTI-CURRENCY, MULTI-DEVICE

Multi-currency billing. Works on phones and tablets.

READY ON DAY ONE

Pre-loaded with 39 business categories — food, beverages, accommodation, supplies, and more. No setup. Open the app and start selling.

From a corner shop to a mountain lodge, a city café to a busy bar — Billmate is the POS and billing app that keeps running, even offline.
